♧   Project Overview

This capstone investigates how observed search performance signals can be used to prioritize pages for content refresh or improvement.

Research question
Which pages should be prioritized based on their observed performance signals?

Workflow

  • Review and prepare performance data
  • Compare baseline and ML approaches
  • Rank content refresh opportunities
  • Communicate findings for decision support
