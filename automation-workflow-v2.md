# Ship an Automation Workflow v2

## Workflow: AI Study Notes Generator

### Objective
Generate well-structured study notes for B.Tech subjects using AI, reducing manual effort and improving consistency.

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1: Input
Enter the subject or topic (e.g., DBMS, Operating Systems, Machine Learning).

### Step 2: Research & Summarization
Use ChatGPT to generate:
- Key concepts
- Definitions
- Examples
- Important interview questions

### Step 3: Review
Review the generated content for correctness and simplify difficult explanations.

### Step 4: Formatting
Format the notes into Markdown with headings, bullet points, and examples.

### Step 5: Output
Save the final notes for revision or upload them to GitHub.

## Estimated Time Saved

- Manual preparation: ~40 minutes per topic
- AI workflow: ~10 minutes per topic
- Time saved: ~30 minutes per topic

---

## Known Limitations

- AI responses should always be reviewed.
- Technical examples may need manual verification.
- Human review is required before final use.

## Tools Used

- GitHub

## Conclusion

This workflow helps create consistent study notes quickly while keeping a human review step for accuracy.
